Publishing every language of a Single at once now runs the update hooks a save of it runs, once the publish is durable: the Single's own `afterChange`, every `afterUpdate` hook heard on all types, and its fields' `afterChange`. Plugins that follow saves (a search index, content dates, a problem check) now see a publish of every language. `publishAllLocales` on a Single also takes `context` and `request` for those hooks.
