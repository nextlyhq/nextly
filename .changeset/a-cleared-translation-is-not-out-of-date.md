A translation whose field was cleared is no longer reported out of date when its source changes. A
cleared field holds no value of its own (the language reads it from another), so it is not judged
by the language's date stamp; on MySQL and PostgreSQL the translation overview and the "stale"
filter reported such a language as stale once its source was saved again.
