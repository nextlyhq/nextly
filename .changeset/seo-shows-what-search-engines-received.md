The SEO plugin can show what search engines actually received. Its new **What search engines received** screen fetches one of the site's pages as Googlebot, LinkedIn, X and Slack and shows each crawler's title, description, share image, official address and search listing beside what Nextly holds, with every difference numbered and explained in plain words. A page's SEO tab links to it. Only the site's own production addresses can be checked: an address on another site, on another port, with a user name or password, or resolving inside a private network is refused before anything is fetched, every redirect is held to the same rules, and a fetch stops after 10 seconds, 5 MB or 5 redirects.
