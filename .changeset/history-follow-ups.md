A plugin's history surface is stable: `ctx.services.versions` and its version pins (`PluginVersionsService`, `PluginVersionPins` and their types) are `@public` in the plugin SDK, now that a first-party plugin depends on them. A sheet that is not modal lets Tab leave it even when nothing inside it can take focus. On a phone, moving between a version's changes from the bottom bar says where it landed, as the wider bar does.
