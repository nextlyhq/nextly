Sign-in and the other auth routes, for a client whose address cannot be resolved, now read at most as many request bodies at once as the shared ceiling admits. A body was read before the request counted against anything, so a client could hold any number of slow bodies open below the ceiling. A request past that number is refused with a 429 before any of its body is read, and the refusal spends no budget and no ceiling place.
