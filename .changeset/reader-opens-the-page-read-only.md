An entry or a Single now opens for a user who may read it but not change it, on the same page an editor gets, read-only: a banner says their access lets them view it but not change it, and to ask an admin. Saving, discarding a draft, duplicating, previewing and sharing a preview are not offered, while unpublishing and deleting each still follow their own permission, and history and the id stay. Such a user makes no claim on the document, so nobody is told they are editing it. A custom edit view is handed the same read-only state through `documentLock`.
