The page builder's canvas outlines the block under the pointer, tagged with its name as Layers shows it, as hovering its Layers row does; nothing is outlined while a block is dragged. The block toolbar keeps to what is on screen: it moves below a block just under the top of the screen, and stays pinned at the top of the screen over a tall block whose top is scrolled away. Alt+F10 moves focus to the block toolbar, from the canvas or from text being edited. The spacing handles take a press across 24px, the minimum target WCAG 2.2 asks of a pointer control, inside the band each one edits, so a press just past it still selects the neighbouring block or the block's own child.
