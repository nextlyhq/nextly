Turning translation on or off keeps what the moving column was. A migration that turns translation on or off records it in the registry of every database it reaches. The column that receives the values takes the moving column's default, and its type where that is wider than the field's own. On MySQL, a required main column made nullable as translation starts keeps its default, comment, character set and collation. A move that would lose a column's comment, character set or collation is refused before anything runs, on a push, a Builder save, a field group's update and `nextly migrate`.
