A registered widget may declare a Collection setting, and its card reads the collection the reader chooses. The collection becomes the card's query source in place of the one the widget declares, and the reader's access to it is judged as for any source a query names.

Registration still refuses a Filter, Sort or Group by setting on a registered widget (`WIDGET_CONTENT_SETTING_NOT_DRAWN`), since nothing yet carries those into its query.
