Masking email addresses in a log line now costs time in proportion to its length. A quoted address was read from every double quote with no limit, so a long line of escaped quotes cost its length squared, and text an attacker could put into a log line could hold a production server's CPU for seconds. A quoted local part is now read for at most 64 characters between its quotes: RFC 5321 caps a whole local part, quotes included, at 64.
