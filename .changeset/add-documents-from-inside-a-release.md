A release's page has **Add documents**, for an ordinary release you may add to. It opens a picker across every collection and Single with a Draft/Published lifecycle that you may publish or unpublish, with a search, a collection filter and **Has unpublished changes**. Pick several documents and add them in one step, going live or coming down; the release takes all of them or none.

A document already in the release, or one you may not publish (to go live) or unpublish (to come down), is listed but cannot be picked, with the reason beside it; changing the action judges the list again.

Once documents are added to go live, the dialog lists the documents they link to that will not be live when the release runs, and offers to add them to go live too. **Not now** adds nothing more. Documents added to come down are offered nothing.
