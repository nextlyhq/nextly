A field group's or Single's create refused over tables nothing registered owns names a remedy that clears it. A view reading the leftover is refused with its own reason, `create-table-viewed`, under `CREATE_BLOCKED_BY_LEFTOVER_TABLE`, and is dropped or redefined rather than renamed. A table referencing the leftover drops its reference, or the leftover itself is renamed. Rows a Single left in a shared field-group table are named apart from the leftover's own tables, and only those rows, with the rows nested beneath them, are deleted, never the table, which holds other entities' rows.
