A tenant's brand colours no longer make a dashboard chart unreadable. Where branding sets the chart colours, the admin checks each against the card, a bar's track and the tooltip, in light and in dark, and moves the lightness of one below 3:1 until it reads, keeping the brand's hue and never refusing it. The chart colours are now written per mode. `@nextlyhq/ui/color` gains `readableOn` (`@experimental`), the adjustment itself, with the target ratio as a parameter.
