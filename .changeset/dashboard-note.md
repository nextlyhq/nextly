A reader can put a Note on their dashboard: Markdown of their own, private to their layout, drawn read-only with no HTML and links held to http, https and mailto. Widget settings gain an experimental `textarea` type with a `maxLength` counted in characters (code points) by one counter, `textLength` on `nextly/config`, used by the settings sheet and the layout write alike. `offered` gains an experimental `"on-request"`: a card added from the add panel only, never placed by default. A dashboard over its size limit is told "This dashboard is full: remove a card or shorten a Note".
