An entity transform judges a plugin named `code` like any other plugin: another plugin transforming its entities needs `code` in `dependsOn`, and may not remove or change the storage of its fields. A plugin named `code` can also no longer contribute an entity under the slug of an app entity of another kind. A transform that restates the owner's table settings in an equivalent form is now accepted: an explicit `status: false` or `localized: false` where the owner left it unset, or a `dbName` that resolves to the table the entity is already stored in. A setting that changes the table, or turns a status column or localization on or off, is still refused.
