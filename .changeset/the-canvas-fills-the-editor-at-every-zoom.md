The page builder's canvas now fills the room the editor gives it, at every zoom. The frame used to stand at a fixed device height (800px on desktop), so most of a long page sat below a short window. Now the frame's width is the device's, fitted to the room, and its height is the room's at that scale, so a taller page scrolls inside it, as in a visitor's browser window, and `vh` on the page answers for that window. A host that states a `height` for `CanvasFrame` keeps the old behaviour: the frame is laid out at that height and fitted both ways.
