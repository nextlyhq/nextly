Deleting a component now tells someone who may change a document, but not
publish it, the right thing: "You don't have permission to publish it."
instead of "change it". That happens where converting the component's
copies would write the document's live copy, such as a live translation of
a Single that keeps drafts. Someone who may not change the document at all
is still told "You don't have permission to change it."
