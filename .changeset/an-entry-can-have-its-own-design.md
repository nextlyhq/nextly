An entry can be given a design of its own: `give-entry-design` copies its collection's published template into a draft that names the entry, and `remove-entry-design` takes it away, through the page builder's template actions route, the composition service and `@nextlyhq/builder/ops`. An entry has at most one own design, held by a unique key on every dialect; it is deleted with its entry. Own designs are stored in the `templates` collection with an empty `collection`, so a collection's template is still found by its collection, and the `collection` field is no longer required by the schema (the guard requires it of a collection's template).
