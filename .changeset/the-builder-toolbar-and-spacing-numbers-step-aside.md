In the page builder, the block toolbar no longer hides the block you want to click. It fades while you point at any block it covers, above, beside or below the selected block, and a click there selects that block instead of pressing a toolbar button you cannot see. Pointing at the selected block brings the toolbar back, and its buttons are reached from there as before.

The spacing overlay no longer hides a small block's label or color. A number thicker than its band shows only while you point at that band, and a block that holds no blocks, such as a Button or a Heading, shows its spacing as outlines with no fill, so its own color looks as it will on the page. Containers keep their filled bands.
