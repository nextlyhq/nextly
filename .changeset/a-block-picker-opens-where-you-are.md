The page builder has a small block picker that opens where you are. Press "/" on the canvas, with a block selected or nothing selected, and it opens beside that block with a search, the six blocks you add most (Heading, Text, Image, Button, Box and Columns until you have added any) and "Browse all blocks and sections →". A block goes where the Add panel would put it, a line says where, and the new block opens for typing when it has text. Help lists "/" as "Add a block here". Plugins can open the same picker at a place of their own through `@nextlyhq/builder/shell` (`InsertPicker`, `useInsertPicker`, experimental).
