A sign-in, first-run setup or other signed-out form whose submission is refused now says so in the form. When the page is open at an address the app does not accept, for example `127.0.0.1` for an app at `localhost`, it names both addresses and links to the same page at the app's own. Before, the form looked unchanged. The auth routes' 403 `CSRF_FAILED` answer now carries `data.reason` (`"origin"` or `"token"`) and, for an origin, `data.appOrigin`; what they accept is unchanged.
