Arrange handles three more stored layouts as an author expects.

- Stack on a device now also stacks a narrower device that holds a row of its own, and keeps its order.
- A shared style's layout is read through every device the page's styles are written for, two at one width included.
- A flex direction with no plain reading, such as `inherit`, reads as Arrange: Custom, and Use plain controls clears it.
