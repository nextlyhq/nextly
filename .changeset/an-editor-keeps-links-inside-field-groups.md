Saving an entry or a Single in the admin keeps a related item or upload inside a field group or a dynamic zone that the editor may not read, or that no longer exists. The editor sees it as a "Restricted or missing item" with its id, which can be removed but not opened, as at the top level; before, a save removed such links. A read at `depth=0` now returns a to-many relationship inside a field group as an array of ids, as for every other field, rather than as its stored JSON text when the field's name is camelCase.
