Each row of the Email hub's Activity list opens a detail sheet. Its state comes first: what happened, why in plain words, and what to do for each kind of failure. Then come its details: the template, which version of it was sent (the owner's default, the site's version, or that none was recorded), the provider, the recipient kind, and the provider's message id. Last is every attempt with the provider's structured answer, and a queued email also shows its next attempt and whether its message is still held. When a queued email failed because of its template, Fix template opens that template for a reader who may edit it. On a phone the sheet fills the screen.
