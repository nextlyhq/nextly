Taking the live shared values into a pending change now applies to the document the caller was checked against: a Single replaced since is refused as not found, and its pending change is left alone. A field rule judging whether a value may be taken now sees the whole pending change as taking would leave it, so a rule that reads another field, such as a price writable only while a lock is open, answers for the real document rather than for the taken fields alone.
