A collection publish whose pending change edits a field the publisher may not write, inside a group the publisher sends with the publish, is refused again and the pending change is kept. The field gate keeps that protected value at what the live row holds, so the publish had read the change as no change at all, published, and deleted the draft with the edit in it. A protected value the pending change adds inside such a group is refused the same way. A publish that sends a group with a protected child at its live value, where the pending change left that child alone, now publishes rather than being refused.
