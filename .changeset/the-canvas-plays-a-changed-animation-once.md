On the canvas, a block's **Animation when it appears** now plays once, on that block, each time you change it, and **Play**, beside the setting in the Style tab's Effects, plays it again. Otherwise the canvas still shows every block in its final state: nothing animates as you scroll or as the page redraws. A block plays with its own effect, duration and delay, and by time whichever start it has, so "When scrolled into view" can be watched too; the blocks inside it stay still. When your device asks for reduced motion, neither a change nor Play animates anything. The published page is unchanged.
