A collection's `delete` access rule may answer a filter instead of `true`. A delete through the Direct API or a plugin service then removes an entry only if it matches the filter. An entry outside the filter is left in place and refused: with a 403 where the caller may read it, and as a missing entry (404) where it may not. The entry is matched under the delete's lock, so a concurrent change can't move it out of the filter between the check and the delete. A filter on a translated field is matched in the site's default language, since a delete removes every language.

Over REST, and for the bulk and batch deletes, a delete rule's filter is still refused, as before.
