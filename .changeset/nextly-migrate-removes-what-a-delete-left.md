`nextly migrate` now removes what a deleted collection, Single or field group left in the tables every entity shares, once its table and its registry entry are both gone: its field-group values, its archived translations, its translation provenance and its translation transition record. Before, a delete applied by `nextly migrate` dropped the entity's tables only, and an entity created later under the same name found these as its own. Each entity's rows go in one transaction, and each one cleaned is named in the run's output. Every later run removes what an earlier delete left, deletes made before this release included.
