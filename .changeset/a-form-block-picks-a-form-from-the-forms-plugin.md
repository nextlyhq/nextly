A Form block picks one of the site's forms from the Forms plugin as its first setting. The list marks draft and closed forms, and a picked form shows where its fields, emails, spam protection and saved responses come from, with "Edit this form ↗" and "or use a simple form". The canvas draws a picked form as visitors will see it, and says when it's a draft, closed or deleted, with "Pick another form" for the last two. Without the plugin, the block edits its own fields with a hint about what the plugin adds, and a simple form with no "Send to" address says on the canvas that its answers have nowhere to go.
