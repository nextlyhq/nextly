A collection update that runs in a transaction (`updateMany`,
`updateEntryInTransaction`) and is held as a working draft now hands its
after-hooks the draft it wrote, as the ordinary update does. Before, `afterUpdate`
hooks, code-defined and stored alike, and the fields' `afterChange` hooks were
handed the unchanged live row, with no sign that the edit was a draft: a hook
reading `status` saw a publish, and read the old content as the new.

The hooks now get the pending draft marked `_isWorkingDraft: true`, with
`originalData` the prior draft on a repeat save, and the update answers with
the same marked document.
