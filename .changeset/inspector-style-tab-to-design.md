The page builder's Style tab follows the editor design. The block card sets the block's mark on a tile, with the name over a one-line description. Section headings are compact, with their "has values" dot beside the name and the chevron pointing right while closed and down while open. Margin and padding are one box model, margin around padding, with each side a compact field that takes a value typed with its unit and is named "Margin top" or "Padding left" for the element's axes. Font weight is one list of named weights, Thin 100 to Black 900 and the relative weights, and line height takes "1.5", "24px" or "150%", each without a separate "form" choice. A field's provenance dot shows when the field is pointed at or focused, and the value hints are short enough to read inside their fields.
