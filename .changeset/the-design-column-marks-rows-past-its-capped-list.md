The entry list's Design column now marks an entry whose own design lies past the capped collection list: where that list may not be the whole, the column looks up the rows on screen by their ids in one request per list page (`GET /templates/entry-designs?collection=<slug>&entries=<id>,<id>`, at most 100 entries, read as the caller). "Remove own design…" checks the removal with a dry run as its confirmation opens, shows any refusal and offers the removal only once the check passes. Deleting a draft collection template now says its entries stay unshown rather than that they stop being shown.
