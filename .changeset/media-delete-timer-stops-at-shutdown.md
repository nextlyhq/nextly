A media delete's Undo-window timer now stops when Nextly shuts down. Before, a delete made shortly before `shutdownNextly()` (or a test instance's teardown) still ran its timer afterwards, against services that were gone or belonged to a newer instance, and logged a "could not be finished" warning after shutdown. The delete is still finished by the job it queued and by the next library read.
