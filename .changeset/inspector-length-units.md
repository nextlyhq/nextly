The page builder's length fields take a bare number in the unit they show. A Style field holding a length shows its unit menu even while empty, on px unless the author chooses another, and "24" is written as 24px rather than refused as "not a length"; a typed unit ("50%") still wins. The property's keywords, such as auto, are listed in the menu after the units. The field and its unit read as one control, the unit a small suffix at the field's end.
