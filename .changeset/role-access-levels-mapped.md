Internal groundwork for the roles screen's per-collection access levels, with no change to what anyone may do. The admin gains the mapping between the levels the roles screen will offer (No access, Can view, Can edit, Can publish) and permissions; nothing uses it yet. In `nextly`, the rule for which built-in resources' legacy `manage` permission still counts as read, create, update and delete moves into a module of its own, unchanged.
