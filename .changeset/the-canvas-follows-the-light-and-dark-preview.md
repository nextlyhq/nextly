The page builder's canvas now follows the Light and Dark preview in the Design panel. Picking Light or Dark draws the page in that mode on the canvas, whatever the device prefers, and a tag over the canvas says so: "Dark preview · Use device". "Use device" goes back to the page's own mode. The choice lasts for the editing session only and is never saved, so a reload, the published page and its Preview are unchanged. A page the site serves into the canvas is shown with that colour scheme too, so its own `prefers-color-scheme` styles can follow the choice in browsers that support it. When a component variant is previewed as well, both tags share one row.
