The admin now sends the version an editor's copy started from with every save of an entry or a Single: a save, a working-draft save, publish, unpublish, publishing every language, a restore, discarding a pending change, taking shared values live, and a list's bulk actions. The form builder and the form submissions list send it too. A save refused because someone changed the document since opens a conflict dialog that names who changed it and when, keeps the edits, and offers to load the current version (the edits stay recoverable), to save anyway after a confirm, or to compare the two versions. A recovery point keeps the version its edits began from, and its offer says when the document changed since.
