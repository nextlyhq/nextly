A field group's settings save that removes a translated field while it adds a nested field group is now refused before anything changes, as the collection and Single settings saves already refuse it (`TRANSLATED_FIELD_REMOVED_BESIDE_ADDED`). A nested field group stores its rows in its own table, so the save's own check saw no column added beside the removed one and let it through, and the removed field's translations column was dropped with every language's value. Rename or remove one translated field and save, then add the others.
