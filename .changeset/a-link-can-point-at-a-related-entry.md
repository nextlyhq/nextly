A link can now point at the entry a to-one relation names. Bind a link's entry to the relation's id, such as `author.id`, and set its collection to the relation's collection: a post's byline links to its author's page. The id is read from the related entry as visitors read it, so an author they may not read, or one not published, gives no link, and the block keeps its own address. A relation to many entries, or to entries of several collections, is still refused.
