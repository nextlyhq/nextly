A page binding that follows a relation into the media library, such as an image's alt text bound to `featuredImage.altText`, now shows that media item's field. The page's read-ahead reads the item through the media library, as the page's images are read, where before it asked for `media` as a collection, failed the read and showed the fallback. `BindingSourcesArgs` in `@nextlyhq/blocks-react/next` gains an optional `media` reader; without one, such a binding still shows its fallback.
