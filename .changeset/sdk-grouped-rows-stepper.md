Plugin pages can draw more of the admin through `@nextlyhq/plugin-sdk/admin` (experimental). `ListView` and `DataTableView` take `grouping`, which draws rows in groups under a header row with a title, a badge, a count and actions (folding the rows away with `collapsible`), and `rowDetail`, which gives a row a toggle that opens a region below it. `Stepper` shows where a flow of a few steps is, with a way back from a step already done; the email provider setup now draws its steps with it. `FormActions` takes a `message`, such as "1 page changed", in place of "You have unsaved changes".
