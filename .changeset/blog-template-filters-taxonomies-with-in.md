The blog template's category and tag pages list their posts again. They filtered a post's categories and tags with `contains`, which a has-many relationship now refuses with a `400` (`FILTER_OPERATOR_NOT_FOR_LIST`), so those pages, the post counts beside each category and tag, and a post's related posts came back empty. They now filter with `in`, which matches a post whose list holds the id on every database.
