In the builder's Pages panel, each header & footer set's ⋯ menu offers Open, Rename in place, and Duplicate, which makes a draft "Copy of" the set with a slug of its own. Rename is offered to an author who may change sets and Duplicate to one who may make them; each write is checked against that right before it runs, and written as the author.
