The SEO plugin no longer calls a structured data detail missing when it is mapped to one of the dates every entry holds by itself. SEO › Content types and an entry's SEO tab now judge a mapping to `createdAt`, `updatedAt` or `firstPublishedAt` as the page's structured data does, and the Content types screen offers Created and First published as sources for the date published and a video's upload date. A mapping into the plugin's own SEO group is no longer flagged either. A page not saved yet says when such a date will be set. `plugin-sdk` adds `entryDateFieldNames` (experimental), naming those dates from core's own declaration.
