The Schema Builder no longer gives a relationship stored as a list (`hasMany`, or one pointing at several collections) a foreign key over its JSON column, and a relationship whose `relationTo` names a different collection from its `options.target` is no longer keyed to the `options.target` table, since reads follow `relationTo`. Every place that creates, adds, predicts or alters a relationship's key reads the same rule.
