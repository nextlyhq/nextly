A Section's background can lay a colour over its image, with a strength in percent, under the content; it is set per device like any background value and never darkens an image nested inside. A Section's Size section offers Height: Auto or Full screen (`min-height: 100svh`). A Button's Size section offers Small, Medium and Large, which scale the button's own padding tokens and its text size, and Full width. A design token reference may carry `times`, a factor from 0.25 to 4 for a length or a number, written as `calc()` over the token under the site's prefix. A Button's label is centred in it, as a `<button>`'s is, whether it renders as a link or not, so a full-width or wider button reads the same either way.
