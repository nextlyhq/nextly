The content route draws a link to a page or an entry only where a visitor may read the target, so a published entry kept from the public by its collection's read access no longer has its address published in a link. Pre-rendering lists the entries' addresses alone beside the pages the pages route lists, so a site with many pages still pre-renders its entries within `staticParamsLimit`. A published template pointed at another collection, after its own was renamed or deleted, now serves the new collection's entries in the same save.
