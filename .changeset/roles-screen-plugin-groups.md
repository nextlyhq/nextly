The roles screen folds each plugin's collections under the plugin's name, closed at first, with one word for what a role holds across them (a level, or Custom). Open, a plugin shows a level row for each of its collections and a switch for each permission it declares of its own, such as the form builder's Export Submissions. A plugin that declares permissions but contributes no collection gets its group too. Those permissions leave the grid below, which keeps the remaining settings permissions.
