Settings and the Email hub are granted by plain permissions. `create-settings`, `update-settings` and `delete-settings`, `update-email-providers` and `update-email-templates` are seeded, and settings create and delete are no longer hidden from the permission list. Each settings and email gate asks the permission its action names, so a role ticked "update settings" can change the general settings, the languages and the sidebar. Image sizes ask their own action on settings rather than `manage-settings`. A schema change (a collection's or a Single's definition) asks all three of create, update and delete on settings. A role holding a `manage-` permission keeps every one of these, since it still counts as the plain four.
