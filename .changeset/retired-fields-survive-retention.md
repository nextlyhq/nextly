A removed component field now stays retired even when its retirement record and the repair after it both fail, however many saves follow. The Page Builder holds the published versions either side of the removal against version retention until a repair records the retirement, then releases them. Before, enough later saves could prune the version from before the removal, and with it the only evidence that the field had been retired.
