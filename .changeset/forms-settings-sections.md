Form settings are one page in sections: Form, After someone submits, Availability, One response per person and Spam protection. The form ID is fixed once the form has been live, a form can carry an internal note that only people who manage forms can read, and the closed message is always at hand in Availability. Settings now stores `oneResponse` and `botCheck` in place of `allowMultipleSubmissions` and `captchaEnabled`; forms saved with the old keys read as before, and the old keys are still accepted. The per-form reCAPTCHA site key is no longer offered, since a key works only with the site-wide secret.
