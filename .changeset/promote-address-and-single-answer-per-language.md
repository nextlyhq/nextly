`nextly db:sync --promote` prints the page builder declaration of a promoted collection's template address again: it reads the address from the template's per-language row, where every installation now keeps it. A Single's update answer, and the document its afterUpdate hooks receive, hold a translatable group (such as an SEO group) as an object again rather than as stored text. The page builder's Design panel stops looking for a group to reveal or focus once the panel has closed.
