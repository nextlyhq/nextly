A Single's or a field group's delete on PostgreSQL or SQLite is all or nothing, as a collection's already is. Its translations, its tables and its registry entry go in one transaction, so a failure part way leaves the whole Single or field group in place, and its delete migration is withdrawn rather than kept for `nextly migrate` to finish. MySQL, which commits each table drop as it runs, deletes step by step as before.
