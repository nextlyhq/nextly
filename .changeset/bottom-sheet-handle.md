On a phone, the document panel and an item's media details open as bottom sheets with a grab strip: dragging it down past a quarter of the sheet, or flicking it, closes the sheet, and a shorter drag springs back; Escape still closes it. Each sheet is as tall as its content, up to 90% of the screen, and scrolls inside beyond that. `SheetContent` from `@nextlyhq/ui` gains a `dragHandle` prop for bottom sheets. No drawer library is added.
