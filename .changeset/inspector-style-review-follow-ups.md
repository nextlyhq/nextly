In the page builder's Style tab, a line height steps with the arrow keys as a number or a length, and reads CSS's whole number grammar, so `1e2` is a number. A font weight stored as text, such as `"700"`, shows as its own choice and can be repaired by picking a weight. A spacing side holding a design token cuts the token's name to the side and shows its Clear button over the box while the side is pointed at or focused.
