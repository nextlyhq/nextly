A document is translated on a page of its own. Each field kept per language is shown beside its source, taken from any other language whose text for that field is published and up to date, the default language unless the translator picks another. A field with no such source is written as original content. Saving writes that language alone, and the fields every language shares are named but not edited there; where no drafts are kept, the page says before a save that it changes what readers see at once. The language is published from the page after being told what its readers will see, the fields another language stands in for among it, and can be taken offline again. Leaving with unsaved work asks first, naming the language, and the page says when someone else has the document open. Addresses that opened the editor in another language, and the links in the list, the dashboard and the translations list, now open this page.
