The page builder's canvas helps fill a page where it is empty. An empty page shows a "Start your page" card with an Add section button, and takes a dragged block anywhere on it. A "+" in the gap between two top-level sections, shown when the pointer is near the gap or on keyboard focus, adds a section there. An empty box says "Drop a block here, or click to add one". The block toolbar takes the pointer on its buttons only, fades while the pointer is over another block, and keeps its height clear when a block is scrolled or focused into view.
