A `where` filter on a has-many relationship or upload now matches by membership, on every database, through REST and the Direct API. `in` and `equals` find entries related to any of the ids. `not_in` and `not_equals` find entries related to none of them, including entries with no related entries. Before, `in` and `equals` compared the whole stored list and found nothing, and `contains` matched the list's text, so one id could match part of another. `contains`, `search` and `like` are now refused on such a field with a `400` (`FILTER_OPERATOR_NOT_FOR_LIST`) that says to use `in`.
