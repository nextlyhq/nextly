A form submission saved inside a transaction sends its notification email only once that transaction commits. A rolled-back submission sends nothing and records no delivery; a committed one sends and records the email once. Outside a transaction the email is sent at once, as before.

Collection hooks gain an experimental `afterCommit` on their context: the effect it is handed waits for the outermost transaction the write runs in to commit, is dropped if it rolls back, and runs at once outside a transaction.
