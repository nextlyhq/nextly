A history restore made through the Direct API or a plugin carries its request, its hook context and, on a trusted call made for a person, that person as its actor; a Single's restore now passes them as a collection's does. A trusted history list pages as REST does (default 25, at most 100, a malformed page size refused), and naming a version through the Direct API on a checked call answers it as the caller may read it, every field they may not read withheld. A checked call on a Single is judged before its row is looked up. A restore of what the draft already holds is recorded. A save's restored-field sources keep the versions they name but no longer keep the save's own row past the history cap. A rename whose restored fields cannot be judged is refused before the name is written, and the history list judges each page's restored fields against the schema read once.
