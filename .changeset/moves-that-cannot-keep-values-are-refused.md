A Schema Builder move that cannot keep its values is refused with a message that says why: a unique field into a repeater names the repeater, a move into or out of a Blocks field says it holds blocks declared in code, and a move into or out of a field group names the field group and every collection, Single and field group using it. A unique field moved out of a group onto its entity's row is now moved, its column made unique: the save counts repeated values first, and its migration stops before it changes anything where two entries hold one value. A host's save that adds, removes, edits or reorders a field group's fields is refused, naming the field group, whose fields are changed only in its own editor.
