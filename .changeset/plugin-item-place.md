A plugin can now choose where its own item goes: the top bar's right end (the default, unchanged for every existing plugin), the rail's foot just above the Inbox, or the person's menu under "Help and community". The bar and the rail's foot take a component that stays mounted on every page and at every width; the menu takes a declared item with a label, an icon, and either a link or an action, never a component. Set it with `contributes.admin.header.place`; the new types are exported from `nextly` and `@nextlyhq/plugin-sdk` as experimental.
