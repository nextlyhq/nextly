The editor reads what its binding controls need through two new Page Builder routes: `/binding-fields`, the fields a binding can read for the person editing (the entry's, each Single's and the site settings', one relation step in), each marked by whether visitors may read it, with a preview; and `/unbind-plan`, an unbind planned against the document being edited by the same decision the page actions API takes. `ContentServices` gains `listBindingFields` and `planUnbind`.
