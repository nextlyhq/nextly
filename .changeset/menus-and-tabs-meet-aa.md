Accessibility: a page builder block's context menu, toolbar menu and card menu no longer
hide the rest of the editor from assistive technology while open. The shared tabs draw
the open tab's label in the text colour and keep the brand colour as its underline, so a
mid-tone brand colour no longer fails contrast on any admin screen's tabs. A page builder
relation search on a title the user may not filter on now finds nothing instead of failing.
