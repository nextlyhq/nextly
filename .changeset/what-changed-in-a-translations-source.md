A translated field can be reviewed against its source (`@experimental`):

- What changed in the source since the translation was made: `GET .../source-change?locale=&field=` of an entry or a Single, or `nextly.translations.sourceChange`. A source whose earlier text was not kept says why.
- Marking the translation as still right against the source as it is now: `POST .../keep-current`, or `nextly.translations.keepCurrent`. It needs the right to change the field in that language and to read its source, by a signed-in person; the text and its origin stay as they are.
