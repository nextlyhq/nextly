The SEO plugin's structured data now dates a page's `dateModified` by when its published content last changed, the same date its sitemap entry's `<lastmod>` uses. Before, it used the entry's own last save. An SEO-only save, such as importing old SEO values, therefore moved `dateModified` to the import time on every imported page while the sitemap kept the honest date. A field that says when a page changed, such as a correction or review date or the field a content type maps, still comes first. A page with no content date kept yet still uses its own last save, as its sitemap entry does.
