A Form picking a form from the Forms plugin is drawn again when the author comes back to the builder, wherever the page draws it: inside a component the page places, or in the header or footer of its Layout, not only on the page itself. A form published, closed or deleted in its own tab no longer stays drawn as it was until the builder is reopened.
