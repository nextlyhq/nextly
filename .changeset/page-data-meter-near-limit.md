The page builder's top bar shows the page data meter only once a page nears its limit: "Page data: 430 of 500 · what's this?" in amber from 80%, red with "· over the limit", or "Page data: couldn't count · Retry". Page settings always shows the count, and its explanation is in plain words.

A site sets its page data limit once with `pageBuilder({ maxQueries })` (`@experimental`): the editor's meter counts against it, and page routes that set no `maxQueries` of their own render under it.
