In the Schema Builder, an entity's settings are pending changes like its fields. Each changed setting is counted and listed, Undo, Redo and Discard all take a settings change back, and the save's review lists the settings it changes. A save of the settings alone is reviewed first too. A saved address is shown fixed, saying what changing it would break (for a field group, how many places use it), and no longer follows a renamed entity's name. An entity not saved yet keeps its address editable, checked as it is typed, and a new address keeps its fields and what can be undone.
