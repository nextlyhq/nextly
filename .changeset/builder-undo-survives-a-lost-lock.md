In the Page Builder, undo and redo now still work after the document's lock is lost and taken back, for example after the connection dropped or after taking the document back from a colleague. The editor starts with an empty history only when the page itself changed meanwhile, such as when someone else's saved version was loaded, so undo never puts back a page from before their change. For builder hosts, `useEditorState` takes an optional `keeping` argument to carry its history across a remount of the same document (experimental).
