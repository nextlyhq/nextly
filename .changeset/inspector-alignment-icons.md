The page builder's inspector sets alignment and direction with icons. Justify content, align items, align content, flex direction, flex wrap and text align are each a radio group of icons with one tab stop, named in words and shown in a tooltip on hover and focus. The icons follow the container's flow: upright in a column, start and end swapped along a reversed direction, and mirrored in a right-to-left element. The values without an icon, such as `safe center` or `first baseline`, sit in a small list beside the group, where a stored one shows.
