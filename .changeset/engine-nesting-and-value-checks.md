Block documents are now checked against where each block says it belongs: a block that declares the containers it may sit inside is reported when it sits anywhere else, including at the top level of a document. Validation also no longer skips its per-value checks on a document whose stored form merely differs from the document in memory, so problems in those documents are reported instead of silently passing.
