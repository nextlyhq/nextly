Form notifications are queued in the transaction that saves their submission, so a submission and its notifications are kept together or not at all, and marking a submission as spam cancels and clears its notifications not yet sent in the same transaction. One the provider is sending at that moment finishes: it ends sent if the provider takes it, and cancelled if that attempt fails. A submission flagged as spam still queues nothing, and marking it "Not spam" sends nothing.

The `form-builder.beforeEmail` hook now runs inside that transaction: a hook that throws refuses the submission, and it may run again if the transaction is retried. No collection or field changes, so there is nothing to migrate.
