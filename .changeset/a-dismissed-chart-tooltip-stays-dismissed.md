A chart's dismissed tooltip stays dismissed until the pointer moves. Escape hides a hovered tooltip on a dashboard chart, but the bar under a pointer that had not moved could bring a tooltip straight back, because hiding the tooltip uncovered that bar and the browser reported it as entered. Now only a move of the pointer, or of the keyboard's point, shows a tooltip again.
