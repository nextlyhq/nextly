In the page builder's inspector:

- A component placement's field is one row: its name with where its value comes from under it, the control, and the reset.
- A spacing side's value fits its cell (up to seven characters, such as "0.95rem"), and the whole value is in its tooltip.
- A segmented choice's selected option is marked by bolder text in the foreground colour, and a lighter fill in dark.
- The Content tab's rich-text field is drawn as a field, at least a target's height.
