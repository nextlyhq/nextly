A relationship made in the Schema Builder that points at Users now gets its foreign key to the users table, on PostgreSQL, MySQL and SQLite, as a relationship to a collection does. Before, a relationship naming Users by `options.target` was keyed to a `dc_users` table that does not exist.

Behaviour change, for relationships created or added from this release on: deleting a user that a required relationship still points at is refused before anything of the account is removed, with a message naming the collection, the field and how many entries point at it. An optional relationship to Users is emptied, and the account is deleted.
