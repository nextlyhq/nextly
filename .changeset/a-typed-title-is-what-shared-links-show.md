A title or description typed in a page's SEO tab is now also what shared links show (Open Graph and X cards), ahead of the social text the page's route sets in its own code. Many routes copy their heading, such as "Blog", into `openGraph.title`, which hid the title typed for the page on Facebook, LinkedIn and X. The social card's order is now: the social text typed for the page, the title or description typed for it, the route's social text, the Page Builder design's, and else the page's title or description. Where nothing is typed for the page, the route's own social text is still the one shared.
