A plugin's reads of its own collection on a transaction it holds (`listEntriesInTransaction`, `findEntryByIdInTransaction`) take `lock: "update"` (experimental) to read their rows for update on PostgreSQL and MySQL, for a read that goes on to write them, so two transactions doing so take turns instead of one being refused.
