Importing a design-token file reports what the reader passed over from the reader itself, rather than from a second walk of the file. Two of the format's own fields now say what was lost: `$extends` (the group it would inherit from is not followed, so those tokens are not imported) and `$deprecated`. A token the importer refuses no longer also claims its value was read from `$value`.
