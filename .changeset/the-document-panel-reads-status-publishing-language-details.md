The editor's document panel now reads Status, Publishing, Language and Details. Status says where the document stands in the language being edited, as the bar's status does; Publishing names the release the document is scheduled in and when, or says it is not scheduled. The panel and the preview pane share the right side: opening the preview folds the panel, and showing the panel closes the preview. Below 1000px of editor width the panel opens over the fields when asked for, with its own close button, and below 600px it opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen, with History, the preview and the panel toggle in the document's More menu. The panel toggle is now named "Show document panel" or "Hide document panel".
