A one-to-one relationship made in the Schema Builder keeps its column unique again, on PostgreSQL, MySQL and SQLite: the migration a collection's or a Single's Builder save writes now creates `uq_<table>_<column>` for it, as the Builder's own table creator always made the column `UNIQUE`. Since collections, and then Singles, began saving through a written migration, such a column was given a plain index and took two entries naming the same target. A one-to-one saved that way gets its unique index on the next save of its collection or Single, or from `nextly migrate:create`; where its column already holds duplicates, that migration is refused until they are resolved.
