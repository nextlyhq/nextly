A card's quick setting (a `select` or `checkbox` its widget marks `quick`) is drawn in its header and offered in a new card menu at rest, and a change there is saved at once for the reader. Only that card is asked again, and the change is announced once the card shows it. Settings saved outside customise mode are now kept for a reader who has never arranged their dashboard, who goes on following the default. Changing one card's settings no longer reloads every card on the dashboard.
