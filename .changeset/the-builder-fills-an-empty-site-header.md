Where the site's default header & footer set has no header or footer, the builder now draws a dashed **No header yet** (or **No footer yet**) band in its place on the canvas, on pages that follow the default; the published page shows nothing there. **Create a header** makes a new published header with the starter header's design, puts it in the default and opens it for editing in place, through a new route, `POST /default-layout/areas/:area`. **Use a component** puts a published header you choose there. Both need the right to update and publish header & footer sets, and anyone else is told who to ask. A site that draws its header and footer in code shows no band.
