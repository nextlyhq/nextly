The SEO plugin's full site check, which Check again and opening its Overview or Problems screen once a day run as one request, now reads the address record, each page's languages, the structured data settings and the content dates for a batch of pages at once, rather than once for each page, and writes each page's row without looking it up again. What it finds is unchanged. At 2,000 pages the check takes about 5 s on SQLite (was about 9 s), 30 s on PostgreSQL (was 51 to 69 s) and 26 s on MySQL (was 59 s); what remains for each page is the write of its row.
