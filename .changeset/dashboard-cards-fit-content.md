Dashboard cards are as tall as their content, at rest and in Customize: no card is drawn at a fixed number of rows, nothing inside a card is cut off, and the cards that share a row are drawn as tall as the tallest of them. A chart over time is taller on a wider card. While a card reloads it keeps the height it was last drawn at, so the cards below it stay put. The card menu no longer offers a Height. A widget's `size.h` and the `h` in saved layouts are kept and still decide how many list rows a load asks for (`rowsThatFit`); they no longer set a card's height. Nothing stored is rewritten.
