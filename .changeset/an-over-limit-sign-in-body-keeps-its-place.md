Sign-in and the other auth routes, for a client whose address cannot be resolved, now count a request body larger than the limiter reads among the bodies being read until its handler has finished with it. The limiter stops reading such a body early and the handler reads the rest, which the client could hold open, so these bodies escaped the bound on how many are read at once. A request the limit refuses still gives its place back at once.
