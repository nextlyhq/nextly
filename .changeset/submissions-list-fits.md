The Form Builder's submissions lists fit their box and wait for what they need. A form's Submissions tab no longer shows every form's columns for a moment before its own: the list stays loading until the form and the person's rights are known. A list too narrow for its columns, as on a laptop beside the Forms panel, leaves some out instead of scrolling sideways: every form's list gives up its Summary, then names the form under the sender, and a form's tab shows fewer of its first fields until the person picks columns themselves. Someone who can only read submissions gets no tick boxes, since a ticked row would offer them nothing, and the delete question answers "Keep it" for one submission.
