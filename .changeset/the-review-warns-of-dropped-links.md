The Schema Builder's review warns before a save drops a many-to-many's links. A relationship made a single link from a many-to-many loses the table holding its links, and every link in it. The review used to call that change safe, or not a change at all. It now names the change, calls it destructive, counts the links it drops, and says so: "Changing 'tags' from many-to-many to a single link drops its link table and the N links it holds." A single link made a many-to-many is named as a change too, but not as a destructive one.
