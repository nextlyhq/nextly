Duplicating an entry now copies a draft the caller may read. Before, a duplicate made through the REST route or with `overrideAccess: false` read only published rows, so a never-published draft answered 404 even for its author. The source is still read as the caller: a draft their read rule excludes answers 404, as a missing id does, and the copy is still checked as a create. A published entry's pending changes are not copied; its live content is.
