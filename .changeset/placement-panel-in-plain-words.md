The page builder's placement panel reads in plain words:

- The rows are headed "Fields".
- Each row says quietly where its value comes from ("From the component", "Set on this page").
- Reset is an icon named for its row.
- Variant is a row like the fields, and the visibility box is set apart from its words.
- A lock is said once, by the card.
- The header's mark is in the component colour, and a long name is whole in its tooltip.
- The "Part of" notice after a double-click offers Edit component.
- The detach and duplicate dialogs speak of the page.
- A chosen image shows its thumbnail, its file name and its alt text, in the Content tab and on a placement.
