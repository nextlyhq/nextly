Settings offers one response per email address, comparing a chosen Email field that must be required and not hidden by a condition. The builder will not delete that field, make it optional or give it a condition while it is chosen, saying why each time, and an Email field can be chosen from its own panel. Saving a form through any door is refused when its one response per email has no such field. A field renamed before it is saved keeps the choice.
