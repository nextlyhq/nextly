Page Builder pages now carry their structured data in their HTML, and their links to other entries are in the visitor's language. The Page Builder routes place the JSON-LD the content and Single routes work out for a page before its blocks, so a site needs no script element of its own. On a route under a `[locale]` segment, a block's link to another entry is read in the request's language and points at that entry's address in it; a route given its own `resolveEntryPath` is handed the language as a third argument.
