In the Schema Builder, a condition whose source a change has broken is now marked and holds the save. A condition is broken when its source has moved off its level or been deleted, is no longer a type a condition can compare, or no longer has the dropdown option the condition compares with. It stays on its field, never dropped. The outline marks the field, the Conditions tab says why, and the save is refused until the condition names a field it can read or is removed. The check runs on the fields as they stand, so it follows every change, undo and redo.
