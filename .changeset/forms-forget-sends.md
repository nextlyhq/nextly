Deleting a form submission forgets the notifications it sent, in the same transaction: one not yet sent is cancelled and cleared, and the delivery records of one that went out are erased. This holds for one deletion, a bulk deletion, and deleting a form, which now deletes its submissions with it.

The plugin records what each submission sent in a new internal `form-submission-sends` collection. Existing installations receive it through their own migrations: run `nextly migrate:create` and then `nextly migrate` (or `nextly db:sync` in development). Notifications queued before the upgrade were not recorded, so deleting their submissions does not forget them.
