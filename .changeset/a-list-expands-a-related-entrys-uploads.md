A list read now expands a related entry the way a read by id does, to the depth asked for. At depth 2 or more, a post's related author comes back with its own upload (an avatar, say) as the media object rather than a bare media id, and with its own relationships expanded to the remaining depth, each related entry still judged by its own collection's access and field rules. The list still reads each related collection once per level, whatever the number of entries. An unset to-many relationship still reads as `[]` at the top of a list, as before.
