Adding Nextly to an existing app no longer writes a media or health route where a route handler the app already has answers that URL through a dynamic segment, such as `app/api/[...rest]/route.ts` or `app/api/[name]/route.ts`: the new, more specific route would have taken those requests from it. The route is reported, with its content to add, instead. Pages reached through a dynamic segment, such as a site's catch-all page, still leave the routes free to be written.
