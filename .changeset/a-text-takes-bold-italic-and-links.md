A Text block takes bold, italic and links, edited on the canvas: select words and a small bar offers B, I and Link (or press Ctrl/⌘+B, I or K). A Text stays one paragraph, so Enter adds a line break, and a pasted heading or list keeps its words with a note. Text now stores one paragraph of words with those marks (block version 2); a Text saved before reads as its words, and each line break it held is now drawn as one. Its field in the inspector updates the canvas as you type.
