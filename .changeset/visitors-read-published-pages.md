Page Builder pages are served to visitors again. Since visitors are judged by the Public role, a collection with no read rule is not served to someone who is not signed in: boot grants the Public role a collection's read only where the collection's read rule answers `true`, so a site serving its pages through `createBlocksPage` answered every visitor 404. The plugin's `pages` collection now declares that anyone may read it. A visitor still sees published pages only: a draft page, and the unpublished edit of a published one, stay unseen, whatever the request asks for. Your own collections that visitors read need the same: a read rule that answers `true`, or the read granted to the Public role in the admin's Roles screen.
