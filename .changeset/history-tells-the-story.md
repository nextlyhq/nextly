The admin's version history now tells what happened to a document. It shows a timeline of versions and events (created, saved, published, unpublished, restored, named, a draft discarded, and versions removed by retention) under day headings, with who did each. Saves fold under the publish that included them. The current draft and the currently published version are pinned at the top. The list opens on the language being edited, with a choice of every language, and filters for everything, publishes, named versions and events. The most recent rows name the fields they changed. A publish or unpublish now records the version it produced, and publishing a held draft records its move from draft.
