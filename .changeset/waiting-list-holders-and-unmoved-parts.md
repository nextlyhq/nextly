The waiting list (`GET /admin/api/workflows/waiting`, `nextly.workflows.waiting`) lists a part no step has moved yet where its collection's workflow starts in a review stage: a document created there waits for whoever may approve it, in each language its reader may read, before any review state is written. Its cursor (`before` / `meta.nextBefore`) walks these parts and review states together, each once; a cursor from an earlier version is refused with `VALIDATION_ERROR`. On MySQL, a role assignment read by an id in another case now answers with the stored person's roles, and whether a person or role holds a step is decided by the same permission decision as every other check.
