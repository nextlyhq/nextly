A page whose Collection list could not hold its later pages when the site claimed its existing pages at start-up now waits for them, and holds them once the page in their way is unpublished, moved or deleted; the waiting report lists it at that later page. A list whose split into pages is bound to the entry holds its later pages too. A development reload no longer ends the start-up coordination of page saves before its own pass finishes. `mayPaginateList` in `@nextlyhq/blocks-engine` (experimental) answers whether a stored document may page a list.
