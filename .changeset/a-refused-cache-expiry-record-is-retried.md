When the database refuses the record that tells other instances a cache tag expired, nextly now retries it by itself until it succeeds: after 1 second, then waiting twice as long each time up to 30 seconds, and at the process's next cached read, where a timer may not run. Other instances see the write within about a minute of the database answering again, instead of only after the process's next write. It logs once when the retries start and once when they succeed, and the timer stops when nextly's services are released.
