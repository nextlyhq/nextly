A translated document's languages are one button in its header, Languages, beside History. It counts the translations that are out of date or hold unpublished changes, and its menu says in one line where each language stands, with Translate for each translation and Edit for the default language, Publish other languages and Manage languages. The Languages card in the side panel and above the fields is gone, with its Copy from language, Open and Start from buttons. A side panel section a plugin places by `language` keeps its place.
