In the Schema Builder, the Unique option on a collection's built-in title now saves. Turned on, the title is checked for repeats first: the save is refused, naming how many entries share a title, where any do. Turned off, the title may repeat again. A saved setting shows again when the collection is reopened, and Discard all takes back an unsaved one. A collection's slug is always unique, through an index every collection table has, so its Unique switch now shows that, on and not switchable.
