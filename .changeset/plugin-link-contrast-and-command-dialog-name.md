Accessibility fixes: small links in the Page Builder's dialogs and panels, the SEO import page and the accounts screens are drawn in the text colour and underlined, so they stay readable under any brand colour; and the `@nextlyhq/ui` `CommandDialog` names itself for screen readers, "Command palette" unless a new `title` prop says otherwise, with an optional `description` prop, so the admin's and the builder's command palettes are no longer unnamed dialogs. A `CommandDialog` given its own `DialogTitle` or `DialogDescription` as a child should pass the words as `title` and `description` instead, since the dialog now draws its own.
