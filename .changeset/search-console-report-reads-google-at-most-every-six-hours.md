The SEO plugin's Search Console report on **SEO > Settings > Connections** now loads in seconds rather than a minute or more. It asks Google for its counts and inspects the most visited pages all at once, where before it made about fourteen requests one after another, and it keeps the report for six hours, reading Google again only after that, so opening the tab again shows it straight away. The tab says when the report was read. Connecting again or disconnecting forgets the kept report. One click now reads "1 click", not "1 clicks".
