A retried cache tag expiry record now writes only to the ledger: it no longer expires the tag in the instance a second time, so a page regenerated since the write stays cached through a database outage instead of being rebuilt after every retry. A refusal kept while a retry is under way waits the first retry delay again once that retry succeeds, and the tags a cache expiry hand-off gave back after its timeout are retried too, rather than waiting for the next write.
