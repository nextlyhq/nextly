A page of any type can now carry an FAQ section in its structured data. Declare it for one page with `faqSection: true` in a route's `structuredDataHints`, or for a whole content type with the new "Questions on its pages are an FAQ section" switch in SEO › Settings › Content types (or `structuredData.types.<type>.faqSection` in code). The page keeps its own type, and its questions (from the route, or its FAQ field) are described as an `FAQPage` that is part of the page. Pages that declare nothing are unchanged.
