The Needs attention card links to exactly what it counted. A grouped widget answer may carry `asOf`, the instant it was computed at, and the card measures its "publishing this week" link from that instant and the source's own window (`ATTENTION_DUE_WINDOW_MS`, from `nextly/attention-keys`) rather than the browser's clock. A Single's part key may carry the language its pending change was counted in, and its link opens that language.

A kind whose lists were only partly read, with nothing found in those that were, now reads "Not all checked" rather than being left off the card, so the card never says all clear about lists it did not read. The source caps its per-collection parts at the widget limit and marks the answer `truncated` when more exist, and the small card links a spread kind's two largest lists before saying how many more there are.
