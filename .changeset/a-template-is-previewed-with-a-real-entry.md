A template is edited against a real entry of the collection it draws. A Previewing strip pinned above the canvas names the entry and its status, and opens a picker of the entries the author may read, drafts included, newest first and searchable, with New to make one in a new tab. Shift+Alt+Down and Shift+Alt+Up step through the list. The canvas's bindings, lists and conditions read the previewed entry as the author, and with no entry to preview they draw their fallbacks. A template's lists and conditions are judged at save against the collection it draws. `BuilderShell` gains a `canvasStrip` slot, and `@nextlyhq/builder/shell` exports `PreviewedEntryProvider`, `usePreviewedEntry` and `PREVIEWED_ENTRY_KEYS`, all experimental.
