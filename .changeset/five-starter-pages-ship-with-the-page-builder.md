The page builder now ships five starter pages, offered in the New page dialog and on an empty page: **Landing**, **About**, **Contact**, **Pricing** and **Coming soon**. Each is built only from core blocks, with every colour, space and type size one of the site's design variables, so a page made from one follows the site's colours, fonts and Site text in light and dark. Their words describe an example business for the author to replace, their Images start empty, and Contact and Coming soon each hold a Form whose **Send to** address the author sets. A site that lists only its own starter pages passes `pageBuilder({ builtInStarterPages: false })`.
