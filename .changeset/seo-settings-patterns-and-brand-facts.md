The SEO plugin has an SEO settings screen: first-time setup asks for the site's name and production address, what kind of site it is and what AI tools may do, and a logo with a share image; the settings keep the site's defaults, a title pattern per content type set by clicking its variables with a live preview, and one record of brand facts. Page heads read them, a save expires the cached heads, and the site's name is never added after a title that already ends with it. `readSeoRules`, `readBrandFacts` and `seoSettingsCacheTags` are exported.
