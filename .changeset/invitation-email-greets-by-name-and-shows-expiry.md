An accounts invitation email can greet the person by name and say when its link stops working. Its template now receives `name`, `expiresAt` (ISO 8601, UTC), `expiresAtText` (such as "30 November 2026, 5:00 PM") and `timeZone`, and the default template uses them. The time is written in the new `timeZone` option of `accounts()` (an IANA name such as `Asia/Karachi`), UTC when it is not set. Inviting the same address again sends the new link's expiry.
