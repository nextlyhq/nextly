In the data picker, a folded row such as **Site settings** or a Single no longer carries an expanded state a list option may not have. A screen reader now reads what choosing it does, "Shows its fields here.", as the row's description, and its name stays the Single's. This fixes a critical accessibility finding in every picker that folds Singles · Site.
