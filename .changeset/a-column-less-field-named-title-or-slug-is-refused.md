A code-first collection or Single can no longer give a field that stores no column of its own (a component, a many-to-many relationship or a virtual field) the exact name `title` or `slug`. `defineCollection` and `defineSingle` add their own text `title` and `slug` beside such a field, and the two shared one document key, so a write could be lost. Such a configuration is now refused when it loads, naming the field: rename it, or declare `title` or `slug` as a text field to replace Nextly's own. A name in another letter case (`Slug`) and a field inside a group or repeater are accepted as before.
