The builder can wrap selected blocks in a Box, and unwrap a Box, Section, Column or Card while keeping what is inside. "Wrap in a box" puts blocks side by side into a new Box at their place, in order; "Unwrap (keep what's inside)" puts a container's blocks at its place and removes it. Both are in the toolbar's menu, the right-click menu and the editor's search, each is one undo step, and a result the placement rules forbid is refused with the reason.
