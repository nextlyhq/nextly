Plugin menu items show in the admin's sidebar again, on desktop and in the mobile drawer. A plugin's `contributes.admin.menu` entries (the Page Builder's Patterns, Components, Layouts, Templates and Bindings to fix, among them) are listed in the panel they belong to: Plugins, Collections, Singles, Settings or the plugin's own section, and each panel's search filters them with its other entries. The dashboard header also counts plugin attention items: it no longer says "You're all caught up" while the Needs your attention card lists one.
