A many-to-many relationship keeps the table holding its links when its collection is saved in the Schema Builder. The Builder used to send such a field back without the settings that make it a many-to-many, so a save of its collection either was refused (a relationship stored the earlier way, naming its collection only in its settings) or turned the field into a single link and dropped the table with all its links (a relationship made through the API, or one whose collection was picked again). The Builder now sends those settings back unchanged. Picking a different collection keeps the field a many-to-many of the new collection; its links, which named entries of the old one, are dropped.
