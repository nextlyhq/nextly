A form's notification rules, which name the addresses each submission is emailed to, are no longer returned to visitors by the public form endpoints (`GET /api/forms` and `GET /api/forms/:slug`) or to staff who can only read forms. Staff who can update forms and super-admins still read them, and submissions still send every notification. The form's internal note follows the same rule, and super-admins now read it too.
