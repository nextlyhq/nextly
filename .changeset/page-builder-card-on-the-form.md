The Page Builder card on an entry's form draws the page inside its header and footer, with its images and the header's logo, as the builder's canvas draws them. Its list of sections is folded under "Sections (N)", and its way in reads "Open in builder". On an entry never saved, the card offers "Save draft and open builder" ("Save and open builder" where the collection keeps no drafts) in place of a disabled button: it saves the entry with the form's own checks, then opens the builder.
