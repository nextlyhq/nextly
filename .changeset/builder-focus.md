In the Schema Builder's outline, Focus shows one group, repeating rows or blocks field on its own, under a breadcrumb from the top level, with every action still on its rows. A crumb or Exit focus goes back with the selected field still selected. A field moved out of the focused container, by key, by Move to… or by dropping it on a crumb, takes the focus with it, so it stays in view and keeps focus. A move is now refused when its field's id names more than one field, and a layout group carrying two names that clash cannot take them to another level.
