A migration can no longer change another owner's table, column, index or constraint in a form the guard cannot read. A column or constraint the same migration adds to a table may be changed later in that migration, and is no longer refused as the other owner's. An ALTER TABLE that names an index, and MariaDB's ALTER KEY on another owner's index, are refused.
