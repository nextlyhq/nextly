The SEO plugin now serves the site's `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt` at the root, both read from each page's own answer: the sitemap lists each published page at the address its type declares, leaves out pages hidden from search or whose canonical points elsewhere, follows the published page for `lastmod`, lists each page's main image and becomes a sitemap index past 50,000 addresses or 50 MB. robots.txt names the sitemap and asks AI crawlers what `aiCrawlers` says, and `hiddenTypes` hides a whole content type. `urlFor` and `basePath` no longer shape the sitemap and are deprecated: declare `publicAddress` instead.
