The Style tab now starts with a "Block style" section: one picture tile per saved style of the block's type, drawn in the site's own styles, the block's own style marked Default and the worn one chosen, with the line "A saved style for every Section." under the tiles. "Edit block styles" there opens the Design panel's Styles tab with that type's block styles opened and in view. The panel no longer calls them "Look". The editing bar sits under the tiles and keeps its size when a block style is worn and a value changed. `InspectorPanel` takes an experimental `onEditBlockStyles` callback, and `@nextlyhq/builder/shell` exports the experimental `blockStyleFoldId`.
