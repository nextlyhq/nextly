The roles screen shows the built-in roles as they are. Super-admin says it has every permission, including new collections and plugins, and offers nothing to change. The Public role, which sets what visitors who aren't signed in may do, offers No access and Can view on each row. A write stays reachable through a row's switches, with a warning beside it, and saving it changes only its permissions. A preset is shown read-only, with "Copy to change" opening a new role that starts with the preset's permissions and description. Roles from the API carry `builtIn: "public" | "super-admin"` to tell these two apart.
