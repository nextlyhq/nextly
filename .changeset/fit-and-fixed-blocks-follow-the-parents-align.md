A block given Fit or Fixed width now stays where its parent puts its blocks, instead of moving to the left: centered in a centered Section, at the end under one aligned to the end. While its width is Fit or Fixed under a Stack, a stacked Row or a Grid, a new Align row under Width offers the parent's place, named after the parent ("Section's", "Box's", "Column's", "Card's"), then Left, Center and Right for this block only, each showing the CSS it writes on hover. Pages built before this change keep their Fit and Fixed blocks on the left, which now reads "Align: Left", until the author picks the parent's choice.
