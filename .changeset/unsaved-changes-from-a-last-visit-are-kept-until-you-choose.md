Unsaved changes from a last visit are no longer lost when a page or entry is opened again, in a new tab or after the old one stopped responding. Until you restore, discard or save, the editor records nothing over them, so an edit made first no longer replaces them. In the page builder, the choice to save or discard them is now shown as the page opens.
