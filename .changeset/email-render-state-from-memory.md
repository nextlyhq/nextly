Sending an email no longer reads the template engine state or the brand settings from the database each time: each instance keeps both in memory, loaded at boot and updated at once by its own changes, and reads them again at most every 30 seconds outside a transaction. On a site with several instances, a template confirmed as Liquid or a brand change made on one instance can take up to 30 seconds to reach the others; until then they send as before. A send inside a transaction never reads either on another connection.
