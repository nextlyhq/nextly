A collection something depends on can no longer be deleted. While a field of another collection, a Single or a field group points at it, a foreign key a plugin or the app declares points at its table, or a plugin or the app has added a column, index, foreign key or check to one of its tables, the Schema Builder's delete, the schema API, `nextly prune --force` and `migrate:create` refuse before anything is removed, naming each with its owner and table and saying what to do. Where those cannot be read the delete is refused rather than going ahead. A Builder bulk delete is now one request checked as a whole, so collections that point only at one another can be deleted together; deleting each alone is refused.
