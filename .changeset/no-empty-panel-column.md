A page with no panel (the dashboard, Releases, Translations) no longer holds an empty 256px column beside the admin's icon rail; its content starts at the rail. A page whose section has a panel (Content, Media, Settings, a plugin's own section) still reserves it from the first paint, decided from the route, so nothing shifts while the panel's links load. Moving between the two moves the content once, as part of the navigation.

A page can now ask for its panel to start folded away with `useFoldedPanel(key)`: the rail stays, a panel toggle appears at the left end of the top bar, and each reader's choice is remembered for that page.
