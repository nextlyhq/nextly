A collection list now reads its entries' relations and links to entries in batches, so what it costs to read no longer grows with its length. A list item's binding through a to-one relation (`author.name`) now shows the related entry's field. Each image is read once per page render, however often the page draws it. A page past its read limit still renders: a list it could not read draws its empty state, and the server logs one warning naming the page, the limit and the blocks drawn without their data. `QueryBudget` reports what a render read, by kind, for the editor.
