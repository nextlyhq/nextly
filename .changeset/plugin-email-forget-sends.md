Plugins can forget the email they sent about something they deleted: `ctx.services.email.forgetSends({ queuedIds, idempotencyKeys }, { tx })` (`@experimental`) cancels the plugin's own queued emails among the ids that are still waiting or retryable, clears the message and recipients of each, and erases the delivery records of the sends that carried the keys, in the caller's transaction when it passes one. `ForgetSendsInput` is exported from `@nextlyhq/plugin-sdk`.
