An editor who gains a document they were kept out of now starts from the copy
saved while they waited. When a colleague leaves the document, their claim runs
out, or the editor takes it over, the entry editor, a Single, a custom edit view
and the Page Builder load the latest version before the form unlocks. Until it
arrives the form stays read-only and says it is loading. If it cannot be loaded,
the form stays read-only, says it couldn't load the latest version, and offers
"Try again". Before, the form unlocked over the copy read while the colleague
held it, so the editor's next save could put that older copy back over the
colleague's work.

The first claim on opening a document still unlocks at once, and an editor who
takes back a document they were displaced from keeps their unsaved changes on
screen as before.
