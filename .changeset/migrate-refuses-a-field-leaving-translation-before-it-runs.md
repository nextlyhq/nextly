`nextly migrate` checks a field that stops being translated with Required or Unique before the move runs. When an entry has no default-language value, or two entries would share one, it refuses with the field and how many entries are in the way, and changes nothing. Before, the move stopped part-way, after its first two files had run. Migration files written this way need this version of Nextly or a newer one.
