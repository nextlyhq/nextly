A new core table, `nextly_cache_tag_expiries`, keeps when each cache tag last expired, on the database's clock. It's where writes made outside a request (a job, the CLI, another instance) will record the tags they change, so every instance can expire its cached reads. Run `nextly migrate` to add it. Until it exists, nextly warns once and works as before.

Rows older than seven days are pruned by a retention pass, in bounded batches, both on writes and on the scheduled webhook drain.
