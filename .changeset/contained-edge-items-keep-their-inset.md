Two more contained-block layouts get the right inset. A contained flex item its author makes fill the row with a `min-width` of 100% or more keeps the containment inset, since it is as wide as the row the inset is measured from: its own `min-width` sets `--nx-fills-row`. And the children of a `display: contents` wrapper, which draws no box, are treated as the items of the layout around it: the wrapper hands them its own flags. Because the compiled CSS changes, every stored page stylesheet is recompiled once, on its next read.
