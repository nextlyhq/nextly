Saved sections in the page builder's Add panel now show a small picture of each section, drawn the same way as the canvas, from the section's published version. Pictures follow the canvas's light or dark preview, appear only as their tiles come on screen so the panel opens at once, and a section that can't be drawn shows a plain icon. The builder exports the picture as `DocumentMiniature` (experimental) for other places that let an author choose a document.
