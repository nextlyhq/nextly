Workflow screens meet WCAG 2.2 AA more fully. A Publish held until approval stays reachable by keyboard, says why in words beside it and to screen readers, and opens "Choose what to publish…" when pressed; a Publish refused for want of a permission stays disabled. Each workflow stage is shown as its words with a shape for its kind (Drafting, In review, Approved, changes requested, a removed stage) in the line under the title, the Review section and the Languages panel, never by colour alone. A step taken, or refused, is announced to assistive technology as well as shown in a toast, and refusals in "Choose what to publish" use the status text colour, which reads at AA in light and dark.
