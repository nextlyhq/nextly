A detail read now reads a populated related entry's translations, and a component's, in the same statement as their rows. Under `experimental.perLanguageStorage` every entity keeps its translatable values per language, and each of these used to take a second statement after its rows: a depth-1 detail read with a related entry and a component made 5 statements where 3 suffice. A component read in several languages at once, a dynamic zone, and list reads still translate their rows after reading them. A list page now takes its version counters with its translated content, one statement where two were sent.
