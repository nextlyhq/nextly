A code access rule that answers with a filter that isn't well formed (an unknown operator, an empty `in`, an `and` that isn't a list, and the other shapes a `where` refuses), or a filter from a create rule or a Single's rule, which may only answer `true` or `false`, is now reported in the log once, naming the rule's collection and operation. In development it is also raised as an error. The caller is refused either way, as before. A rule that answers a well-formed filter is refused as before too, and its log notice now says the check does not apply a rule's filter, rather than telling the rule to answer `true` or `false`.
