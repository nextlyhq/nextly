A Divider's Border section names its line as a line: Line colour, Thickness (its top
border's width) and Line style, with a note that the line is the divider's top border.
Its other sides show only while they hold a value. An Image has a shadow under
Effects, which falls on the picture in its own shape, round on a Circle, whether the
picture is captioned, linked or enlarged. The link and the enlarge button around a
picture no longer clip it: the picture clips its own corners.
