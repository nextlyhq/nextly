A language's review and its history are on its translation page. Where a workflow holds the language, its next step leads the page's actions, its stage is said beside its status, and Publish waits for approval, saying for whom; a step moves that language alone, and is offered to a reviewer who may not write it. With unsaved work, Publish then waits for Save, since a save asks for the review again. History opens on the language's versions and the shared fields. Restoring puts the old text into the page as unsaved work and writes nothing until it is saved; a field every language shares is listed and can't be chosen. An address that opens History in a language other than the default now opens its translation page.
