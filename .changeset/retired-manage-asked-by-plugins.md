A plugin route whose `requiredPermission` is `manage-media`, `manage-settings`, `manage-email-providers` or `manage-email-templates`, or a `ctx.caller.can("manage", resource)` on one of those four, is answered by the plain read, create, update and delete there together: a caller holding all four passes, one missing any of them is refused, and a role `nextly upgrade` has not yet converted passes as before. Each plugin that asks is warned once, naming the permission to ask instead; ask the plain permission the route needs, such as `update-settings`.
