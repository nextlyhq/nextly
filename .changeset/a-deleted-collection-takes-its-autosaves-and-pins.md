Deleting a collection in the Schema Builder now also removes its autosaves, the version pins features held on its entries, each reader's "last looked at" markers and dashboard pins, and the record of the media its entries used. Before, these were left behind, and a new collection created under the same name inherited them. They go with the collection's registry entry, after its tables are dropped, so a delete that fails part-way on MySQL keeps the collection listed with all of them, and deleting it again finishes it.
