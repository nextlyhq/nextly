A site declares how each language's address is formed, under a prefix or on a domain of its own (`languageAddresses` in `nextly.config.ts`), and `languageAddressOf(path, locale)` answers the address a page kept in a language is opened at. Content and Single routes, the blocks routes included, placed under a `[locale]` segment read the language from it: one route serves every language, a segment naming none of the site's languages answers not found, and the addresses a route hands on are formed by the rule.
