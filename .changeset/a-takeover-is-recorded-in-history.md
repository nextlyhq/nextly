Taking over a document someone else is editing is now recorded in that document's history: who took over, from whom and when. The takeover and its event are written together, so a takeover whose event cannot be written is refused and the previous editor keeps the document. Claiming, renewing, asking for access, releasing and a lapsed claim record nothing. History names the previous editor by their current name, read when history is read, never by email.
