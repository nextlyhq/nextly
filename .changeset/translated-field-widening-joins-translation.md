A field that becomes translated is refused for a change of its storage only when the translations table's column cannot hold every value its column holds now. A widening (a longer `varchar`, `varchar` to `TEXT`, a wider number) copies each value as it is. On MySQL, a translated collection with a `title` field could not be saved from the schema dialog: the system `title` column (`varchar(255)`) moving into the translations table (`TEXT`) was refused as a reshaped field.
