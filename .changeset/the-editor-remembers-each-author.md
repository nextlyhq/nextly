The Page Builder now keeps its preferences (the open panel, the device, the zoom, the panel widths and hidden sides) for each signed-in author rather than for the browser, so two people sharing a browser each find their own. The preferences a browser kept before move once to the first author who opens the editor. `@nextlyhq/plugin-sdk/admin` gains `useCurrentUserId()` (experimental), the signed-in user's id or `null`, read from what the admin already knows, and `@nextlyhq/builder` gains `userPreferenceStore(userId)` to pass to `BuilderShell` as its `store`.
