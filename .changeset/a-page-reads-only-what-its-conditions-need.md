A published page reads only what its visibility conditions need. A list whose item conditions order a number, or match text or a yes-or-no, no longer reads the site settings for a time zone it doesn't use, so it keeps its share of the page's query budget. Page metadata counts the entry read a page's conditions make, so under a tight budget it describes the page the visitor gets. A Single's page has no entry for conditions to read, as the editor and the save already say, and the renderer now agrees.
