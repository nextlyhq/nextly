A plugin schedule in the earlier `{ name, schedule, handler }` shape now runs, with a deprecation warning at start-up: `name` is the slug, `schedule` the cron, or an interval in milliseconds of whole minutes dividing an hour or whole hours dividing a day, and `handler` runs as a job of its own, given the plugin's context. One that cannot be mapped, such as a 7-minute interval, a cron that doesn't parse, or one with no handler, is not run, and the warning says why. Neither stops start-up. Declare `{ slug, cron, job }` instead.
