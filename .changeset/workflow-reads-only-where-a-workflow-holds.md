The admin asks for a document's review state, and for whether anything can wait for its reader's review, only where a workflow holds something that reader may read. The signed-in workspace answer (`GET /api/admin-meta/workspace`) now carries `workflowEntities`: the collections and Singles a workflow holds that the reader may read, by slug. An entry of a collection no workflow holds is answered "no workflow" without a request, and a site with no workflow attached sends neither request on a list or an edit page. Where the workspace answer does not say (it failed, or comes from an older server), both are asked as before.
