Form submissions keep the page they were sent from: the origin and path of the
browser's `Referer` (never its query or fragment, and only for `http`/`https`
pages), shown on each submission's page under Details › Page. The plugin adds
one field to the submissions collection, `page` (long text), created on the
next schema apply; earlier submissions keep none.
