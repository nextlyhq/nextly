A block part can now take style properties from a wrapping root. The image's
picture takes its corners, proportion, fit and focal point from a figure (a
caption) or a link around it, from every tier that styles the image: its look,
a shared style and its own values. A captioned or framed image's box, and its
caption, are no longer rounded or squared by them, and the image's look sits
on the root with the other tiers, so a shared style or the image's own values
override it as they do any other property.
