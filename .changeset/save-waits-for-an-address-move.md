In the Schema Builder, Save now waits while the new address of a collection, Single or field group not yet saved is being checked, and says so beside the button: "Save waits while the new address is checked." Before, a save started during that check could create the entity under the address it was leaving, while the editor moved to the new one, leaving a draft behind under the new address.
