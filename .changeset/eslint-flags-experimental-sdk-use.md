`@nextlyhq/eslint-plugin` gains `no-experimental-sdk`, which names each `@experimental` surface of `@nextlyhq/plugin-sdk` a plugin uses, as a warning in `configs.recommended`. A scaffolded plugin picks it up with no change. What counts as experimental is generated from the SDK's stability ledger and TSDoc tags, so a surface that graduates stops being flagged in the next release. By default the rule checks imports; `context: true` also checks plugin-context members such as `ctx.db`, and `allow` lists surfaces adopted on purpose.
