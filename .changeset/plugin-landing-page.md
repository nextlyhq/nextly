A standalone plugin can name the page its rail item opens with `admin.landing`, one of its own pages or collections, and a site can override it in `admin.pluginOverrides`. Without one, the rail item now opens the first collection its panel lists for the reader, skipping a hidden or unreadable one, and with none, the first menu item its panel lists. A landing naming anything else is ignored, and a development start warns about it and about a standalone plugin with menu items but no collections and no landing.
