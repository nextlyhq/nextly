The SEO plugin has a redirects screen, SEO › Redirects: every redirect with who added it, a form to add one by address or by `/old/*` pattern, inline edit and removal, every refusal in plain words, and a Not found tab listing the addresses people asked for that do not exist, most visited first, each one click from a redirect to the closest live page. Import CSV shows what a file would change, row by row, and imports it in full or not at all. An address no language serves by the site's rule, such as an old unprefixed link on a site whose languages are all prefixed, is redirected and logged in the default language. The not-found log counts a request once however many times Next.js renders its not-found page, and its writes no longer try to expire cache tags during that render.
