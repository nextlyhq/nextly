Nextly can now find every link stored content holds, to other entries and to media files, wherever it is kept: fields of every kind, field groups, translations, many-to-many links, version history and releases. It can also point those links at new ids or file addresses in one pass. Links to items that are missing are kept and listed. This is the groundwork for moving content between sites.
