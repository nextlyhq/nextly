The first-party plugins' collections are open to the preset roles again on a new site: Page Builder's pages, layouts, components, patterns, templates and their indexes; the form builder's forms, submissions and their records; the SEO plugin's redirects and records; and commerce's products. Each is opened to Admin, Editor, Author and Viewer, each by its own rule, as before new collections started closed to them, so a new site's Editor works on its pages and forms. A host's `presetGrants` (or, for the form builder, `grants` in a collection override) still decides where it is set. The accounts plugin is unchanged: it keeps people's accounts in tables of its own, not a collection.
