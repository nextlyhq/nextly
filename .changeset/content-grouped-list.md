The admin's Content panel now lists collections and Singles together in one list, each under a heading with its count, instead of switching between them with a segmented control. One search filters both headings, and each heading folds, remembered for you across visits. Collections that set `admin.sidebarGroup` are listed under that group's sub-heading inside Collections. Where no `admin.icon` is set, a collection now shows a list icon and a Single a framed-page icon. A role that can read only one kind sees only that kind's heading, and the panel's title links to the Content overview.
