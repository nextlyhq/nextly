The page builder keeps the canvas's bound-data and list answers for as long as the builder is open, instead of asking again every 15 seconds and on every return to the tab. A new `POST /canvas-sources/changed` route returns one mark for what the canvas reads. The builder checks it when it opens, once a minute while the tab is visible, and once on return. When the mark changes, or the page is saved, the canvas reads again.
