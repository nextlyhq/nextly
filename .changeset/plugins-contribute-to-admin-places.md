Plugins declare blocks, tabs, list columns, row and bulk actions and side panel sections for admin places under `contributes.admin.contributions` (experimental). Each is checked when the app starts (its place, whether the place takes its kind, its fields, its namespace, and its order beside the others there) and sent only to a signed-in admin, already in order, leaving out for each reader what needs a permission they lack or names a field they may not read. The login page's alone reach the public admin metadata. A plugin's entry-list filter options are now offered to each reader only where the list read would let them filter by the option. Nothing in the admin draws contributions yet.
