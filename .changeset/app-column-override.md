The app's schema hook can change how a collection's, a Single's or a component's field column is stored, with `schema.overrideColumn(table, column, col.varchar(10))`. A string field's column may become `text`, `longText`, `shortText` or `varchar(n)`, a whole number a `bigint`, and a decimal may gain digits. Every push and migration keeps the override, a SQLite rebuild included. A string narrowing is judged by the stored data. A change outside the column's value family, a plugin's override, and an override of anything but the storage are refused when the hook runs.
