A plugin can now work out the head of every page the site serves. It declares the `page-metadata` capability and contributes a `pageMetadata` resolver, and the content, Single and Page Builder routes ask it for each page with no change to the site's routes. A route's own `buildMetadata` becomes one input: its title, description and social values are used where nobody set one, and its `robots` and canonical are not used. Without such a plugin, routes serve the same metadata as before. The contract types are in `@nextlyhq/plugin-sdk`.
