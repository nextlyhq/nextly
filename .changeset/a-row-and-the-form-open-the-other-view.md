A collection entry is one step from its other view. Where a collection opens its entries in a plugin view, each list row's menu offers "Edit details", which opens the form; where entries open as a form, the row's menu offers each view the entry can be opened in, and the form's document bar offers it beside History. A document view declares the words of that way in with the new, experimental `openLabel` (for example "Open in builder"); a view that declares none is offered as "Open in" its `label`. Neither is offered to a reader who may not update the entry or in a language other than the default.
