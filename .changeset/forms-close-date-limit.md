A form can close by itself on a date, or once it has a number of submissions. Both are set in Settings › Availability. Past either, a submission is refused with the form's closed message, the one it gives when closed by hand, and `GET /api/forms/:slug` answers as for a closed form. Spam does not count towards the limit. Submissions racing for the last place cannot take more than there are: the limit is held inside the transaction that stores a submission, whatever stores it.
