The entry list now draws what plugins add to it (experimental).

- Blocks stand around the list's own header and list, the place's built-ins `toolbar` and `table`, in the order sent, each behind its own error boundary.
- Declared columns are drawn after the collection's own. A `field` column draws through that field's own cell; a component column draws one cell at a time, each cell behind its own boundary.
- Declared bulk actions are offered in the bar shown while rows are selected, when the action's `visibleWhen` holds for every selected row, and run on the selected rows.
- Row and bulk actions share one filter for the collections they're offered on and one way of running.
