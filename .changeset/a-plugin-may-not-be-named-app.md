A plugin may no longer be named `app`. The site's own permissions are stored under the owner `app`, and a plugin's under its name, so a plugin with that name could not be told apart from the site: uninstalling it, `nextly migrate` or a `migrate:down` could take one's permissions for the other's. Startup and every CLI command now refuse a plugin named `app`, in any letter case, with `PLUGIN_RESOLUTION_ERROR` (`logContext.reason` `plugin-name-reserved`). Compatibility: a plugin named `app` stops the app from starting until it is renamed; `apps`, `my-app` and other names are unaffected.
