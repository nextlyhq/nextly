The Get started card is compact: twelve columns by four rows by default, and as short as three. It draws the whole steps its rows hold, then "Show N more", which grows the card in place to show every step; "Show less" folds it back, and the card is drawn at its own size again on the next load. Nothing is saved by either.

A card that is empty, failed or not available to its reader now shows its message centred in the card rather than at its top-left, so a tall card no longer looks unfinished.
