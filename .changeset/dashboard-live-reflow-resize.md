While customizing the dashboard, the cards move aside as you drag one, so you see where everything lands before you let go, and the card settles into its outline on release; with reduced motion they move without sliding. The drag handle now sits left of each card's title instead of adding a row. On a dashboard at least 1,024px wide you can drag a card's right edge to resize it: it snaps to the widths the widget allows (25%, 33%, 50%, 66%, 75%, 100% and its own limits), shows the width as you drag, and stops at the widest. From the keyboard, a card's menu still moves it earlier or later and says where it went.
