Upgrading an existing installation is safer across databases. On SQLite, a translatable column that moves to its translations table drops its own index first, in one step that leaves nothing half done; an index over it and another column is refused before anything changes, by name. On MySQL, language codes are compared by their columns' collation. `nextly migrate --step` decides what it leaves pending from its own plan, an entity a pending migration removes is no longer refused over, the values move is judged up front, and a refusal after the run's own work names what applied instead of saying nothing changed.
