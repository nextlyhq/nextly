A translation whose source changed is reviewed on its translation page. Under the field, What changed shows the source as it was when the translation was made beside the source now, with the words that changed marked, the current translation, and whether a person edited it. Keep current marks the translation as right against the source now and leaves its text alone; Edit goes to the field. Where the earlier text wasn't kept, the review says so. Nothing is read until the review is opened, and nothing replaces the text unasked.
