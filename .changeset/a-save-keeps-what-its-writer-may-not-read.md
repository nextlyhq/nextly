Saving a group, a repeater or a field group held inside one keeps the values inside it that the
person saving may not read. A container is saved whole, and such a person is served it without
those values, so saving it back used to clear them, with nothing refused and nobody told. In a
repeater they are kept for each row the save keeps, known by its id; a row the save removes goes
with what it held. A value they send there is stored as before.

A write that must be stored exactly as sent is refused where a value is kept in it this way.
