A page saved on a transaction, such as by the SEO import or another plugin's write, is checked for the problem list once that transaction commits, rather than from inside it. The check used to make its own writes on the transaction's connection, where on PostgreSQL and MySQL it could fail the import's apply with `savepoint "nextly_sp_1" does not exist`, and it read the page before the save was committed.
