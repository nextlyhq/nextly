Reconciling a field group no longer refuses one the Builder has just created. A Builder create builds its table through the migration it writes, and reconcile compared its columns with the spelling the earlier creator gave (`VARCHAR(255)` for an email on PostgreSQL, `DATETIME` for a date on MySQL), so a healthy field group was reported as changed. Reconcile now accepts today's spelling and the earlier one, since field groups of both kinds exist, and still refuses any other. No stored data changes.
