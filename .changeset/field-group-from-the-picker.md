The Schema Builder's field picker can make a new field group: choosing the field-group type offers "New field group", which opens the new-entity dialog, checks its address by the server's rule, and holds the group as not saved yet for the collection, Single or field group being edited. The chooser lists field groups not saved yet beside the saved ones, marked so. `FieldPickerConfig.fieldGroups` gains an optional, experimental `onNew`, which a surface sets to make the group itself; the picker chooses the group it resolves with.
