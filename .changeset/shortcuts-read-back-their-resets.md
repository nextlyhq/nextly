A Section's and a Button's shortcuts now read back the value they write over one from elsewhere: Auto shows chosen after writing `auto` over a wider Full screen, and Medium after writing the button's own padding over a wider size. A size reaching from elsewhere is matched to the button's own tokens exactly, under the site's token prefix, so a token whose name merely ends the same way no longer reads as Small or Large. The overlay is offered by the image that wins where it is edited, so an image cleared with `none` there no longer offers one. The editor's style cascade now reports the token prefix its values were written under.
