Selecting a block in the page builder no longer redraws the top bar: the page title, undo and redo, the devices, Preview, the checks count and Save and Publish are drawn again only when something they show changes, such as an edit, an undo or a rename. For builder hosts, `@nextlyhq/builder/shell` adds `useDocumentView`, `useLatestCallback` and `useLatestOptionalCallback` (all experimental): the editor state without its selection, which keeps its identity while only the selection moves, and a handler that keeps its identity and calls the newest one.
