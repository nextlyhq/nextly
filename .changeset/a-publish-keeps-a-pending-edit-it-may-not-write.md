A publish that sends a whole group no longer loses a pending edit to a field inside it that the publisher may not write. The publisher's copy of that field is kept at the pending change's value rather than the live one, so the publish is refused, naming the field, and the pending change is kept. This holds for collections and Singles. A publish whose pending change leaves that field alone goes through as before.
