A plugin can no longer register a kind of signed-in person whose full name is too long to record. Every save by such a person was refused, because a document's history and its "last changed by" record keep a kind in at most 64 characters while registration allowed up to 191. Registration now takes its limit from the narrowest place a kind is stored, so a longer name stops the app at startup with the plugin named, instead of failing each save.
