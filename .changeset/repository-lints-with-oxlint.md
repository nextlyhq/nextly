The repository lints with oxlint and its type-aware rules (oxlint-tsgolint) instead of ESLint. Nothing published changes: the packages lose their own `lint` scripts and ESLint dev dependencies, and two release-readiness checks in the core package derive the collection's status with a type guard rather than a cast. `@nextlyhq/eslint-plugin` stays an ESLint plugin, and projects scaffolded by `create-nextly-app` keep their ESLint setup.
