A link's picker now offers a related entry ("Author › Link to this entry") only where visitors may read it, and lists one whose collection has no page yet as disabled, saying "No page for <collection> yet". A link bound to a related entry is named by its relation ("Author · This post"). Unbinding it keeps the entry's reference, so the link keeps going to the same page whatever its address becomes, and the inspector says so, naming the entry's title where a visitor may read it. The page actions API's unbind answer gains `entry` for such an unbind, and `@nextlyhq/blocks-engine` exports `ENTRY_LINK_PROPS`.
