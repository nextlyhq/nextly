On a database pool of one connection, the usual serverless PostgreSQL setting, publishing a template and saving an entry served under a template address no longer wait for a connection their own write holds until the pool gives up. The published entries a template's publish walks, and the published pages an entry's address could fall on before every page is claimed, are now read on the write's own transaction. The start-up pass over a site's pages reads each page on the transaction that claims it, so it finishes on such a pool, and the entries' pass after it runs.
