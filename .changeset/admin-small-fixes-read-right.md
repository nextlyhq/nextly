The admin's small fixes: counts read "1 draft" and "2 drafts" on the Content overview and the dashboard's content table, through one shared count helper. An entry page's Publishing section and the dashboard's setup card draw their loading placeholders without a block inside a paragraph, so React reports no hydration error. Typing in a field and deleting it back to the saved value no longer leaves a Restore offer for the next visit: the recovery point is emptied once the form is back to what the document stores. Discard on the builder's "Changes from your last visit" offer now lasts across sessions and tabs, while Not now still puts an offer off for the session only. A recovery point that holds no values is never offered.
