A dashboard batch does a source's shared work once. A widget source may answer several of its queries in one call (`resolveMany`, one answer or one error per query, by position) and share work across its queries in one batch through `opts.scope`, kept per source, per batch and per caller. A grouped result can say its counts are floors (`atLeast`), drawn as "N+". The SEO plugin answers its card's five cells from one scan and can group its issues by kind. Both additions are `@experimental`.
