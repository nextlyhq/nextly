A form submission's page keeps unsaved work safe. Answers or a note typed and
not yet saved now hold the page, so moving to another submission or away asks
first; answers typed while a save is on its way stay in the editor; a note
draft is never saved over someone else's newer note; a failed refresh keeps
the page and its drafts instead of replacing them with an error; and after a
submission is deleted the page moves on without asking about it. Fields a
site adds to submissions with `formSubmissionOverrides.fields` show, read-only,
in the page's Details.
