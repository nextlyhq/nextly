The editor tells every language's truth. While the language being edited holds a pending change, the editor's read keeps every configured language's real status, so the Languages panel no longer reads "0 of 2 languages translated". The panel, the document status line and the list's language marks count every configured language, the default included. A Single's status chip and Status section show the language being edited rather than the whole document. "Restore unsaved changes" offers only work typed in the language being edited, and discarding a language's changes forgets the author's unsaved work recorded in that language. A language row in the panel wraps its actions onto a line of their own rather than truncating its markers and state.
