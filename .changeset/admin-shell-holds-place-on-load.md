On a fresh load, the admin's sidebar and header no longer move while they fill in. The sidebar's icons used to be pushed down as the sections the reader's permissions and content decide arrived, and the header's controls slid sideways when the notification bell joined them. The sidebar now draws its icons only as far as is already decided, so each one that arrives later lands below the ones drawn, and the header draws the account menu at once and the controls beside it together once what decides them is known.
