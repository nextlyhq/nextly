The builder's Pages panel lists the site's header & footer sets below its pages. Each set shows a picture of its published header above its footer, its name, how many pages show it with the site default marked, and Draft or Changes not published where they apply. A set's row and New header & footer open the set's form in a new tab. The Pages and Header & footer groups fold, and stay folded per author. The group is hidden from an author who may not read sets.
