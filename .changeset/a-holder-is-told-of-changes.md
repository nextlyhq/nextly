An editor holding a document is told when someone else changes it, naming who and when, within one heartbeat and before they save. The document lock's claim and renewal accept the version the editor's copy started from (`_rev`), and answer `changed` when it moved, with the same rules as a refused save: who and when only to a caller who may read the document. With no unsaved edits the editor loads the current version at once; with edits it offers to load the current version, keeping the edits as a recovery point, or to keep editing. Taking a document back after it changed loads the current version before the form unlocks, and keeps the edits recoverable.
