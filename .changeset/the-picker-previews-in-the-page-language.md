The binding picker previews a bound date or number in the page's language
and the site's time zone, as the published page shows it, rather than in the
browser's. An Arabic page previews Arabic digits, and a date near midnight
previews the day the site's zone gives it. A page with no language, or a
site with no zone, previews in English and UTC, as it renders.

`@nextlyhq/blocks-engine` exports `bindingFormatEnv`, the language and time
zone a render's binding pass formats in, which the editor's previews share.
