A contained block laid out as a flex or grid item, such as two contained Boxes side by side in a row or a contained Column in Columns, is no longer inset from the whole row's width, which over-padded it and made a wide page overflow. It is treated as a sized block: centred within its own share of the row, with the gutter at each side. The rule that sets a block's `display` now also writes `--nx-sized-item` on its children, `1` for flex and grid layouts and `0` for any other, at the same breakpoint and state, and the site sheet's containment inset reads it.
