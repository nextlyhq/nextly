The page builder's canvas now draws under the site's page data limit (`pageBuilder({ maxQueries })`), as the page data meter and the published page routes already do. A site that raised its limit sees blocks past 500 draw on the canvas, as they do on the live page, and a site that lowered it sees blocks past its limit come up empty on the canvas, as they do on the live page. The canvas's bindings are read under the same limit, and a site with no limit (`Infinity`) draws with none. Unset, the canvas still draws under the default 500.
