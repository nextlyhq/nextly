The shared field settings panel can now be configured per surface: narrow the validation rules it offers (`validationRules`), draw a surface's own Conditions tab (`conditions` as a component, given the field's siblings), hide Display's "Read only" and "Hidden" (`displaySwitches: false`), and supply a type's own settings (`typeSettings`). On the forms and user-fields surfaces, the built-in types their pickers offer (such as URL, Phone and File) are known to the panel and named by their catalog labels, rather than showing a loading or load-error state and their raw ids. On every other surface those ids still belong to the plugin that declares them. The Schema Builder's panel is unchanged.
