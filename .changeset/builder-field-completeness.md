The Schema Builder marks a field that lacks what its type needs, as the save would refuse it: a relationship with no collection to link to, a dropdown or radio option with no label or no value, two options with one value, and a group or repeater with nothing in it, beside the ones it already marked. Each is shown on the field's row and at the top of its settings panel, the errors are counted beside the pending changes with a way to each, and Save is unavailable until none stands. Re-saving a select or radio whose options repeat a value is now refused until one is changed; a definition already holding one still loads.
