A field moved into or out of a group in a code-first config keeps its values: `nextly migrate:create` asks about each field that looks moved (or, with no terminal, carries every one with `--accept-moves`) and writes statements that copy its values to the new place, and back in the `DOWN`. A working draft holding the field at its old place stops the migration before it changes anything. Under `next dev`, such a change is held back, naming the field, rather than deleting its values or leaving them where nothing reads them.
