Page checks also find text with too little contrast against its background, below WCAG AA (4.5:1, or 3:1 for a heading), with the ratio. Only colours the author chose on the block or a container are judged, in light mode and in dark mode where the site's variables change them there. Text over a background image or gradient, or under opacity, a filter or a blend, is listed as "Contrast not checked" rather than guessed. `runPageChecks` takes the site's variables as `tokens`.
