Forms can delete old submissions. **Settings › Privacy › Delete submissions after** (Never, 30 days, 90 days or 1 year, stored as `settings.keepDays`) sets how long a form keeps them. A nightly pass the plugin schedules deletes each form's submissions older than that, and spam older than 30 days whatever the setting. Each is deleted the way any deletion is, and a pass that runs out of time continues on the next jobs run.
