`nextly` refuses to delete a collection while a field still points at it, on every path that goes on to drop its table: the Builder's delete, the schema route's delete, and `nextly prune`, which now refuses the whole prune before its first drop. The refusal names each field, in collections, Singles and Field Groups (`posts.tags, the Single settings.featured`), and says to remove those fields or point them elsewhere first, the same on PostgreSQL, MySQL and SQLite. Collections pruned together may point at each other, and `nextly db:sync --promote` keeps working, since the promoted collection's table and slug live on.
