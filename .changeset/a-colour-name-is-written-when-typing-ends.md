In a colour field, a colour typed by name is now applied when you press Enter or leave the field, not letter by letter. Typing a saved colour's name such as "yellow 500" no longer turns the block yellow on the way, and leaving the field on text that is not a colour puts the previous colour back instead of keeping, and saving, the last colour the typing passed through. Hex colours still show on the canvas as you type.
