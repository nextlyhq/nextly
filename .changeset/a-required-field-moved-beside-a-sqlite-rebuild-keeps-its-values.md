On SQLite, a Schema Builder save that moves a required field out of a group while the same save rebuilds the table (a relation added, or another field made required, with or without a value filled in) now keeps every value and makes the field required once they are in, rather than being refused. Undoing a required field's move into a group with `nextly migrate:down`, where that save also rebuilt the table, is no longer refused either.
