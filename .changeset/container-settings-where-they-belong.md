The containers' settings are where an author looks for them, in plain words.
Nothing a page stores changes name, and a page saved before renders exactly as it did.

- **Columns:** the layout moves from Style to Content. It offers six pictured tiles: 2 equal, 3 equal, 4 equal, Wide left, Wide right and Wide middle. Each adds or removes empty columns to match, and writes the row's own values. "Stack on" offers Never, Tablet and mobile, or Mobile. A template stored that no tile writes still reads as the row's own.
- **Box and Card:** a whole-block link can now point to a page or entry on the site, not only an address, and can open in a new tab. Its link relationship sits in Advanced, HTML. A Card's link says "The whole card becomes the link". An address link renders at once, as before; only an entry link waits for the entry's address.
- **Section:** Height reads "Fit content" or "Full screen".
- **Accordion:** "Titles as" sits under Behaviour and offers "Plain text". The icon's position reads "Icon position".
- **Collection list:** its settings sit under Entries: "Show entries from", "Sort", "How many", "Skip", and a "Preview the empty message" switch. It gains an HTML tag (a generic box, a section or an article), and its style options come from the same list every container uses.
- **Entry content:** the field reads "Show field", under Entry.
- **Link control:** a link sibling a block places in another tab, such as a link relationship in Advanced, is drawn there as its own field. A link's hint is drawn under it.
