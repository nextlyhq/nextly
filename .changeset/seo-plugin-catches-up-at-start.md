The SEO plugin brings its address record up to date at start, so a site that had content before the plugin arrived, or whose addresses a deploy moved, is recorded as its pages stand. Where two pages now resolve to one address, the page already holding it keeps it and the other is logged. Plugins taking part in address uniqueness can list the site's content types with `ctx.publicAddresses.types()`, and `held()` now gives each page's `createdAt`.
