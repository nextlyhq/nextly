---
title: PLUGIN_ROUTE_OPTIONS_INVALID
description: "A plugin declares a route option that cannot mean what it says, such as a raw body on a method without one."
---

## Cause

A plugin declares a route option that cannot mean what it says, such as a raw body on a method without one.

A route's options are checked before it is registered: its method, `rawBody`, `rateLimit`, `csrf`, `public`, `principal` and `limitKey`. An option that cannot apply would look like a decision the route does not carry out. The Why line names the option and what is wrong with it.

## Fix

- Correct the option the Why line names in the route's declaration in the plugin.
- If the plugin is not yours, update it to a release that declares the route correctly.

## Example

```text
✗ Plugin "reviews" route GET /summary declares an option that cannot apply.
  Why:  rawBody is only meaningful on a method with a body, not GET.
  Next: Correct the option named in the route's declaration in the plugin.
  Docs: https://nextlyhq.com/docs/cli/errors/plugin-route-options-invalid  (PLUGIN_ROUTE_OPTIONS_INVALID)
```
