---
title: PLUGIN_PERMISSION_DUPLICATE
description: "The same custom permission is declared twice."
---

## Cause

The same custom permission is declared twice.

A custom permission is one action on one resource, compared without regard to case, and the database holds one row for it. Two declarations of it, from `nextly.config.ts` or plugins, would leave that row owned by whichever was seeded last.

## Fix

- Remove one of the two declarations.
- Or give one of them another action or resource.

## Example

```text
✗ The custom permission "export-reports" is declared twice, by `nextly.config.ts` and "reports".
  Why:  A permission is one action on one resource, compared without regard to case, and the database holds one row for it.
  Next: Remove one of the two declarations, or give one of them another action or resource.
  Docs: https://nextlyhq.com/docs/cli/errors/plugin-permission-duplicate  (PLUGIN_PERMISSION_DUPLICATE)
```
