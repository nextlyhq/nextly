---
title: PLUGIN_PERMISSION_SYSTEM_RESOURCE
description: "A custom permission is declared on a resource Nextly reserves for its own permissions."
---

## Cause

A custom permission is declared on a resource Nextly reserves for its own permissions.

Resources such as `users`, `roles` and `media` are Nextly's own, and Nextly seeds their permissions itself. A custom permission cannot be declared on one.

## Fix

- Declare the permission on a resource of the plugin's own, such as one of its collections.

## Example

```text
✗ The custom permission "export-users", declared by "reports", is on a resource Nextly reserves.
  Why:  "users" is one of Nextly's own resources, whose permissions it seeds itself.
  Next: Declare the permission on a resource of the plugin's own.
  Docs: https://nextlyhq.com/docs/cli/errors/plugin-permission-system-resource  (PLUGIN_PERMISSION_SYSTEM_RESOURCE)
```
