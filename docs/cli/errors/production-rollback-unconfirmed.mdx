---
title: PRODUCTION_ROLLBACK_UNCONFIRMED
description: "`nextly migrate:down` was run with `NODE_ENV=production` and without `--yes`."
---

## Cause

`nextly migrate:down` was run with `NODE_ENV=production` and without `--yes`.

With `NODE_ENV=production`, `nextly migrate:down` asks for `--yes` before it rolls anything back: a rollback drops what the migrations added and restores no data.

## Fix

- Prefer rolling forward with a corrective migration, or restoring from a backup.
- To roll back anyway, re-run with `--yes`.

## Example

```text
✗ Refusing to roll back in production without --yes.
  Why:  `NODE_ENV` is `production`. Prefer rolling forward with a corrective migration, or restore from backup.
  Next: Re-run with --yes to override.
  Docs: https://nextlyhq.com/docs/cli/errors/production-rollback-unconfirmed  (PRODUCTION_ROLLBACK_UNCONFIRMED)
```
