---
title: I18N_RESTORE_KIND_INVALID
description: "`i18n:restore --kind` is not `collection`, `single` or `field-group`."
---

## Cause

`i18n:restore --kind` is not `collection`, `single` or `field-group`.

`--kind` says which kind of entity the slug names, when a collection or single and a field group share it. It takes one of three values.

## Fix

- Pass `--kind collection`, `--kind single` or `--kind field-group`.
- Leave `--kind` out when the slug names only one entity.

## Example

```text
✗ --kind must be collection, single or field-group, not "page".
  Why:  `--kind` says which kind of entity the slug names, when more than one kind has it.
  Next: Pass `--kind collection`, `--kind single` or `--kind field-group`, or leave `--kind` out when the slug names one entity.
  Docs: https://nextlyhq.com/docs/cli/errors/i18n-restore-kind-invalid  (I18N_RESTORE_KIND_INVALID)
```
