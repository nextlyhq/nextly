---
title: RESOLVE_MODE_REQUIRED
description: "`nextly migrate:resolve` was given none, or more than one, of `--applied`, `--rolled-back` and `--failed-cleanup`."
---

## Cause

`nextly migrate:resolve` was given none, or more than one, of `--applied`, `--rolled-back` and `--failed-cleanup`.

`nextly migrate:resolve` records one thing about one file per run, and each of its three flags names a different thing to record.

## Fix

- Pass exactly one: `--applied <file>`, `--rolled-back <file>` or `--failed-cleanup <file>`.
- `nextly migrate:resolve --help` describes each.

## Example

```text
✗ `nextly migrate:resolve` needs exactly one thing to record.
  Why:  It was given none of `--applied`, `--rolled-back` and `--failed-cleanup`.
  Next: Provide exactly one of --applied, --rolled-back, or --failed-cleanup.
  Docs: https://nextlyhq.com/docs/cli/errors/resolve-mode-required  (RESOLVE_MODE_REQUIRED)
```
